🌊 Hello from the FloodWatch Team

Hi! We are the FloodWatch Group 🌦️ — a team of developers, dreamers, and weather-watchers trying to help keep your community safe from flooding. Thanks for using our website, it really means a lot to us 💙

📊 What We Do With Your Data (No Creepy Stuff, Promise 😄)

  • Weather data helps our system “think” and predict possible flooding situations 🌧️
  • Your personal info is safely locked away 🔐 (we don’t sell it, ever)
  • We don’t share your data with random strangers — we’re not that kind of team 😅

⚠️ Reality Check (Important Stuff!)

We try our best to predict floods accurately, but we’re not magic wizards 🧙‍♂️ just yet. So please remember:

  • Always listen to official government warnings 🚨
  • Don’t rely on us alone (we’re good, but not superheroes 😎)
  • Keep your emergency plans ready just in case 💼

🔄 Sometimes We Sleep Too (Server Stuff)

We aim to keep FloodWatch running 24/7 like a hardworking guard dog 🐶, but sometimes updates or issues happen. If something breaks, we’ll fix it ASAP!

📱 Your Role in This Adventure

  • Keep your info updated so alerts reach you properly 📲
  • If something looks weird, tell us — we appreciate feedback a lot ❤️
  • Don’t try to hack us… we’re still students 😭

🛡️ Final Disclaimer (Legal-ish Stuff)

FloodWatch is a helpful tool, but at the end of the day, your safety decisions are yours. We provide info, not superpowers 🦸‍♂️. Please stay safe and smart out there!

💙 Final Message

Seriously, thank you for using FloodWatch 🌧️💙 We built this with care, coffee, and a lot of late-night coding sessions. Stay safe, stay dry, and take care!
