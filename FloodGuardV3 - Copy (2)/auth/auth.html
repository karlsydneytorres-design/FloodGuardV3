🌊 Service Overview

FloodGuard provides flood monitoring, prediction, and protection services designed to help communities and individuals prepare for and respond to flood events.

📊 Data Usage & Privacy

  • We collect location data to provide accurate flood predictions for your area
  • Weather and environmental data is processed to enhance our prediction algorithms
  • Personal information is encrypted and stored securely
  • Data is never shared with third parties without explicit consent

⚠️ Service Limitations

While FloodGuard strives for accuracy, our predictions are based on available data and models. Users should:

  • Always follow official emergency guidance from local authorities
  • Use FloodGuard as a supplementary tool, not the sole source of information
  • Maintain emergency preparedness plans independent of our service

🔄 Service Availability

We aim for 99.9% uptime but cannot guarantee uninterrupted service. Critical alerts will be delivered through multiple channels when possible.

📱 User Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate location information for relevant alerts
  • Keep contact information updated for emergency notifications
  • Report any service issues or inaccuracies promptly
  • Use the service responsibly and not for unauthorized purposes

🛡️ Limitation of Liability

FloodGuard provides information services and cannot be held liable for damages resulting from flood events. Users assume responsibility for their safety and property protection decisions.
