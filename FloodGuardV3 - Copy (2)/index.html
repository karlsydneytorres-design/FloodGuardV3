About FloodWatch

FloodWatch is a non-profit initiative dedicated to protecting local communities from the devastating effects of flooding. Our system utilizes a network of advanced sensors to collect real-time data on water levels and rainfall, providing a crucial early warning system for residents and emergency responders.

We believe that with technology and community cooperation, we can significantly reduce the risks associated with natural disasters. By providing timely alerts, accurate data, and essential safety information, we empower the people of Barangay Colgante to stay prepared and safe during challenging weather conditions.
