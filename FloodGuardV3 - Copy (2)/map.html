Evacuation Routes & Safe Zones

Follow these pre-determined routes to reach safe zones safely during an evacuation.

  • ➡️ Route A: Main Road
    For residents of Sector 1 & 2. Proceed towards Barangay Hall.
  • ➡️ Route B: Riverside Road
    For residents near the river. Follow the marked path to the town plaza.

These are the designated locations where you can find shelter and assistance.

  • ✅ Barangay Hall
    Primary safe zone with emergency services and relief goods.
  • ✅ Colgante Elementary School
    Secondary safe zone, equipped for family shelter.
